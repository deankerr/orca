# ORCA documentation

## When to read evidence

- Start with the document owning the product area; dated studies in `docs/evidence` support its maintained conclusions.
- Consult cited studies when investigating a decision's evidence or reassessing its data assumptions.

## Inline notes

- ⚠️ marks a known limitation or issue; describe its trigger and current impact rather than implying urgency.
- 🔭 marks a brief, one-line observation of production data beside the relevant policy.
- Date observations and state scope and units; distinguish direct production measurements from projections.
- Use orca-data-study and `docs/evidence` when an observation needs detailed analysis or supporting artifacts.

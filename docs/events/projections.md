# Projections

- Events are a projection, sharing the concerns of other ORCA/V4 projections.
- Events currently shares entity-owned fact selection with Catalog and pricing selection with Catalog and price history. The extent and specifics of this shared interpretation are still developing.
- Event history normally remains stable. Retrospective regeneration can improve its usefulness as projection rules evolve.

## Globally deferred

- Regeneration and backfill have a pathway through retained observations and explicit inputs. Routine procedures across V4 remain to be developed.
- Projection provenance shares the same cross-area concern: identifying which interpretation produced retained output. Versioning conventions can follow when distinguishing generations becomes necessary.

# ORCA V4

ORCA observes a timeline and derives useful retained state from its scans. V4 brings history,
ingestion, product caches, events, and deployment-local datasets under that model.

## Timeline and knowledge

- The timeline extends indefinitely into the past and future. ORCA fills selected observations into
  its retained history, independently of wall-clock time and ingestion order.
- Scan artifacts are the authoritative captured evidence. Derived history serves products and
  interpretation; fidelity to the complete inputs remains the artifacts' responsibility.
- A retained value is valid as of its scan time. That time dates the observation; upstream changes
  may have happened between observations.
- Entity knowledge is cumulative; endpoint listing state evolves separately from retained values,
  following the [entity terminology](../../CONTEXT.md).
- The earlier scan selects which changes a comparison produces; the later scan dates their values.
  State reads follow retained values by time, with comparison provenance kept in the ingestion log.
- The ORCA clock is computed from the completed ingestion log. An unchanged scan can advance it.
  Partial histories remain useful datasets.

## Pure derivation over a log

- For fixed rules, the supplied scans fully determine their derived output. Stored entity values
  stand independently of previous projections; chronological continuity is a semantic relationship.
- Raw comparison selects retained history. Projections supplies shared extraction, product meanings,
  hydration, and comparison across modules, and can evolve while captured values remain available.
- Ingestion coordinates admission, progress and completion. Its log carries provenance and
  the completed clock; table-owning modules define their retained data and read/write meanings.
- Completion establishes that the admitted work is stored. Historical completeness, cache freshness,
  and event publication have separate meanings.

## Baseline

- A baseline establishes observed state at the start of a selected history. It dates our knowledge
  of those entities, independently of their creation in the upstream world.
- The first real comparison establishes its earlier observation through a dedicated baseline step.
  Its output uses that observation's real time and participates in ordinary historical reads.
- The earliest ingestion owns the baseline. Ordinary event interpretation receives real scan comparisons.

## Independent products

- Capture, ingestion, cache freshness, and event interpretation have separate completion meanings.
  Products can select their own observation time and refresh lifecycle. Caches are mutable derived
  data; event publications are retained derived output owned by their ingestion.
- Current caches rebuild from any prior cache state to the current projection of retained data.
  Historical observation series serve products directly, with distinct retention and read meanings.
- Downstream work anchors to completed ingestions. A shared execution lifecycle prevents conflicting
  runs; each product owns its work and concurrency scope.
- Events treats interpretation as an arbitrary process over its input. Historical context, later
  observations, and judgment belong behind its execution and publication boundary.
- Events publishes its complete output together with processing completion, making retries depend
  on that completion state.
- Published events record selected claims for consumers. Delivery adapters independently select
  broadcasts, primarily by observation time relative to wall-clock time.
- Other deployments select useful portions of the same timeline, by copying retained state or
  reproducing it from the same inputs. Their chosen starting state determines local coverage.

## Design discipline

- Optimize the ordinary forward path. The ingestion gate restricts supported operations; the data
  model tolerates gaps. Additional policies earn their place.
- Initialization and selected deployment histories are first-class uses of the same derivation.
  Exceptional workflows follow from the model and gain automation when useful.
- Existing projections are expendable. Each module conceals its mechanics behind a small interface;
  exact interfaces and organization within V4 develop through implementation. Modules can be composed
  directly within a shared execution while retaining their own responsibilities and completion meanings.
- Start with the minimum schema that keeps the core retained record true. Additional synchronization
  guarantees and operational bookkeeping follow demonstrated product needs.
- Keep this philosophy about meanings and guarantees. Module pages place detailed contracts beside
  their schemas, with open questions and deferred intentions beside the affected topic.
